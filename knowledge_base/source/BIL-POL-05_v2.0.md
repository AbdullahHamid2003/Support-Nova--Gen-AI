# Billing and Payments Policy

Document ID: BIL-POL-05 | Version: 2.0 | Status: Active | Effective date: 2025-11-01 | Expiry date: none | Owner: Billing Operations

## 1 Purpose

This policy sets out how Lumora Home Technologies takes payments, corrects billing errors, reconciles refunds and handles chargebacks and suspected fraud. It ensures that customers are charged only what they agreed to pay, that billing errors are corrected promptly and that payment information is always protected.

## 2 Scope

This policy applies to all payments for orders, subscriptions and Pro Install services made through the Lumora online store and the Lumora Home app. It applies to Billing Operations and to every member of staff who discusses charges, refunds or payment details with a customer. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## 3 Payment Methods

Lumora accepts credit and debit cards and a digital wallet; cash is not accepted. Payments are processed by Lumora's payment processor, and Lumora does not store full card numbers. Every charge is linked to a transaction reference (format TXN-########) that is used to identify the payment in billing complaints. Refunds are always returned to the payment method used for the original charge.

## 4 Billing Errors

Billing errors are investigated using the transaction records held by Lumora and its payment processor.

### 4.1 Duplicate Charges

Duplicate charges are verified against transaction records. A duplicate charge is the same order or subscription charged more than once. Once verified, the duplicate charge is reversed within 2 business days; bank posting can take up to 10 business days. Pre-authorisations that were never captured are explained to the customer and are not treated as duplicate charges.

### 4.2 Incorrect Charges

Incorrect charges are investigated within 5 business days and any overcharge is refunded to the original payment method. An incorrect charge is an amount that differs from the agreed price or an unexpected fee. The customer receives the outcome of the investigation with an explanation of the amount that was due.

### 4.3 Dispute Window

Billing disputes must be raised within 60 days of the charge date; later disputes are policy exceptions that require Supervisor Review. The charge date is the date shown on the transaction record. A late dispute is still registered and investigated, but any correction is decided by the supervisor under ESC-SOP-12 section 4.9.

## 5 Subscription Billing

Subscription billing is governed by SUB-POL-22. This includes plan prices, renewal reminders, auto-renewal and outage credits for Lumora Cloud Vault and Lumora Care+. Cancellations and refunds of subscription charges are governed by the Cancellation Policy CAN-POL-06. Duplicate or incorrect subscription charges are handled under section 4 of this policy.

## 6 Refund Reconciliation

Billing Operations supports Returns & Refunds when a refund that Lumora has issued does not reach the customer.

### 6.1 Payment Trace

When a refund confirmed as issued has not appeared after 10 business days, Billing Operations opens a payment trace. The trace is opened with the payment processor using the refund reference, amount and date of issue. Before 10 business days have passed, the customer is told that bank posting can take up to 10 business days (REF-POL-02 section 4.4).

### 6.2 Trace Reference

The customer receives the payment trace reference within 3 business days. The reference can be given to the customer's bank or card provider to help locate the refund. Billing Operations updates the complaint when the trace result is received and informs the customer of the outcome.

## 7 Chargebacks

A chargeback is a payment reversal requested by the customer through their bank or card provider.

### 7.1 Chargeback Mentions

Customers who mention a chargeback or bank dispute are referred to a Billing supervisor (Supervisor Review). The supervisor reviews the underlying complaint and the transaction records and makes sure that the complaint continues to be handled on its merits. A chargeback threat is recorded under ESC-SOP-12 section 4.12.

### 7.2 No Discouragement

Staff must not discourage customers from contacting their bank or threaten account suspension. Customers have the right to contact their bank at any time. Staff explain what Lumora is doing to resolve the complaint and how long each step takes under the applicable policy, so that the customer can make an informed decision.

## 8 Payment Security

Staff must never request full card numbers, CVV codes, passwords or one-time codes; verification uses the last four digits of the card. Payment details are never written in complaint notes or sent by email or chat. If a customer shares full card details, staff do not repeat them, advise the customer to contact their card provider and remove the details from the record.

## 9 Fraudulent Transactions

Suspected fraudulent transactions are reported to Account Security under SEC-POL-09 section 4.4. Examples include orders the customer did not place and charges made after an account takeover. Billing Operations reverses unauthorised purchases after the investigation confirms that they were not made by the customer.
