# Billing and Refunds FAQ

Document ID: FAQ-BIL-17 | Version: 2.0 | Status: Active | Effective date: 2026-02-01 | Expiry date: none | Owner: Billing Operations

These answers explain how Lumora Home Technologies handles charges, refunds and subscription payments. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. For help with a specific charge, contact us with your order reference or the transaction reference shown on your receipt.

## 1 Charges

Questions about charges on your card or digital wallet.

### 1.1 I was charged twice

We verify the duplicate against transaction records and reverse it within 2 business days of verification; your bank may take up to 10 business days to post it. Sometimes a second amount is a temporary pre-authorisation that your bank releases on its own; if so, we will explain this to you. Please have your order reference ready when you contact us.

### 1.2 The amount charged is wrong

We investigate incorrect charges within 5 business days. If we find that you were overcharged, we refund the difference to the payment method you used and explain how the correct amount was calculated. Please contact us within 60 days of the charge date so that we can review it under our standard process.

## 2 Refund Timing

Questions about when your refund will reach you.

### 2.1 When will I get my refund?

Returns are inspected within 3 business days of receipt; approved refunds are issued within 5 business days of inspection approval; bank posting can take up to 10 business days. We email you when your refund has been issued, together with a refund reference. Refunds always go back to the payment method you used for your order.

### 2.2 Lumora says my refund was sent but I cannot see it

After 10 business days we open a payment trace and send the trace reference within 3 business days. You can give the trace reference to your bank or card provider to help them locate the payment. We will keep you updated through your complaint until the trace is complete.

## 3 Subscriptions

Questions about Lumora Cloud Vault and Lumora Care+ payments.

### 3.1 Renewal reminders

We remind you 7 days before an annual renewal. The reminder shows the renewal date and the amount that will be charged. You can switch off auto-renew at any time in the Lumora Home app or in your account settings.

### 3.2 Refund after renewal

Annual renewals cancelled within 14 days are refunded in full. The refund goes back to the payment method used for the renewal. After 14 days, your subscription continues until the end of the term and is not refunded. Monthly plans are not refunded for part of a month.

## 4 Payment Security

We will never ask for your full card number, CVV, password or one-time codes. To verify a payment, we only ask for the last four digits of your card together with your registered email address or order reference. If someone contacts you claiming to be Lumora and asks for these details, do not share them and contact us through the Lumora Home app.
