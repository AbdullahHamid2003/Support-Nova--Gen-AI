# Professional Installation Service Policy

Document ID: INS-POL-24 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Active | Effective date: 2025-09-01 | Expiry date: none | Owner: Customer Relations

## 1 Purpose

This policy sets out the standards of the Lumora Pro Install service, what customers can expect from an installation appointment and how Lumora Home Technologies handles workmanship problems and property damage caused by a technician. It ensures that installations are safe, professional and put right promptly when something goes wrong.

## 2 Scope

This policy applies to Lumora Pro Install appointments booked through the Lumora online store, the Lumora Home app or support, including installations of the Lumora Aura Smart Thermostat, Lumora Keystone Smart Lock and Lumora Sentinel cameras, and technician visits offered under TEC-GDL-20 section 7. Cancellation of appointments is governed by CAN-POL-06 section 6.1.

## 3 Appointments

Appointments are booked for a date and an arrival window. The customer receives a confirmation and a reminder before the appointment.

### 3.1 Arrival Windows

Appointments have 4-hour arrival windows. The customer is notified when the technician is on the way. If the technician cannot arrive within the window, the customer is contacted before the window ends and offered a new appointment, and no cancellation fee is charged for the missed appointment.

### 3.2 Technician Identification

Technicians carry Lumora photo ID. The technician shows the ID on arrival, and the customer can check the technician's name against the appointment details in the Lumora Home app. Customers may refuse entry to anyone who cannot show valid Lumora photo ID and should report this to support.

## 4 Service Standards

Devices are installed to manufacturer guidelines, tested and demonstrated to the customer. The technician protects floors and surfaces, removes packaging on request and explains how to use the device in the Lumora Home app. Before leaving, the technician confirms with the customer that the installation is complete and records any issues in the visit notes.

## 5 Workmanship Issues

A workmanship issue is a problem caused by the way a device was installed, such as incorrect wiring, poor mounting or incomplete set-up.

### 5.1 Re-Visit

Workmanship issues qualify for a no-cost re-visit within 5 business days. The re-visit is booked when the issue is reported and is carried out by a technician who reviews the original visit notes. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

### 5.2 Installation Fee Refund

If the re-visit does not fix the issue, a refund of the installation fee is considered under Supervisor Review. The supervisor reviews the visit notes, the customer's account of the problem and the result of the re-visit. The Lumora Pro Install fee is otherwise not refundable once the installation has been completed (REF-POL-02 section 3.4).

## 6 Property Damage

This section applies when a customer reports that a technician damaged their property during a visit.

### 6.1 Investigation

Property damage caused by a technician is investigated within 5 business days. The investigation reviews the visit notes, photos supplied by the customer and the technician's account. The customer is asked to keep any damaged items and to provide photos where it is safe to do so.

### 6.2 Claims Review

Damage claims are reviewed by the Department Manager. The Department Manager decides the outcome of the claim based on the investigation, in line with ESC-SOP-12 section 4.10, and the customer receives the decision in writing with the reasons for it.

## 7 Conduct

Technician conduct is governed by STF-POL-21. Technicians treat customers and their homes with respect. Customers can report concerns about a technician's behaviour through any support channel, and the report is reviewed under STF-POL-21 section 5. Allegations of harassment, discrimination, threats or theft by a technician are handled as serious misconduct under STF-POL-21 section 6.
