# Privacy and Data Protection Policy

Document ID: PRV-POL-08 | Version: 4.0 | Status: Active | Effective date: 2026-03-01 | Expiry date: none | Owner: Compliance & Privacy

## 1 Purpose

This policy sets out how Lumora Home Technologies protects the personal data of its customers, including camera footage and audio from Lumora devices. It explains the rights customers have over their data, the rules for marketing and consent, and how privacy incidents are handled, in line with applicable consumer-protection and data-protection law.

## 2 Scope

This policy applies to all personal data that Lumora processes about customers and the members of their household, including account details, order and payment records, device data, camera footage and audio recordings stored in Lumora Cloud Vault. It applies to all Lumora staff, contractors and technicians who can access customer data.

## 3 Principles

Personal data is processed lawfully, minimally, securely and transparently. Lumora collects only the data needed to provide its products and services and to handle complaints, keeps it only as long as necessary and protects it against unauthorised access. Customers are told what data is collected and why in the Lumora privacy notice. Staff access customer data only when it is needed for the task in hand.

## 4 Data Rights Requests

Data rights requests can be made in the Lumora Home app, through the account settings on the Lumora website or by contacting support.

### 4.1 Rights

Customers can request access to, export of, or deletion of their personal data. They can also ask for inaccurate data to be corrected. A request is registered as soon as it is received, and the customer receives a reference for it. Deleting an account also deletes the recordings stored in Lumora Cloud Vault, and the customer is told this before the deletion is carried out.

### 4.2 Fulfilment Time

Requests are fulfilled within 30 calendar days after identity verification. Identity is verified under the Account Security Policy SEC-POL-09 section 6 before any data is disclosed, exported or deleted. When records must be kept for legal reasons, the customer is told which records are retained and why.

### 4.3 Overdue Requests

Overdue data-rights requests are escalated to Compliance Review. A request is overdue when it has not been fulfilled within the period in section 4.2. Compliance & Privacy agrees a completion plan with the team responsible and informs the customer of the new expected completion date, without promising the outcome of any regulatory assessment.

## 5 Marketing and Consent

Marketing preferences can be changed at any time in the Lumora Home app, in the account settings or by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing message.

### 5.1 Consent Required

Marketing is sent only with the customer's consent. Consent is recorded with the date, the channel and the type of marketing the customer agreed to. Service messages about orders, complaints, security and safety are not marketing and continue to be sent after an opt-out.

### 5.2 Opt-Out

Opt-outs are processed within 10 business days; marketing received after that period is a consent breach. A consent breach is recorded as a privacy complaint, the customer's preferences are corrected immediately and the customer is told when the correction has been applied. Compliance & Privacy reviews the cause so that it does not happen again.

### 5.3 No Sale of Data

Lumora never sells personal data; sharing with partners requires explicit consent. Service providers that process data on Lumora's behalf, such as carriers and payment processors, receive only the data they need and are bound by contract to protect it. Data shared without consent is handled as a privacy incident under section 7.

## 6 Camera and Audio Data

Camera footage and audio are among the most sensitive data that Lumora holds, because they can show the inside of a customer's home.

### 6.1 Access Control

Camera footage and audio are encrypted and accessible only to the account owner and invited users. The account owner can add and remove invited users at any time in the Lumora Home app. Shared links to recordings expire automatically and can be revoked by the account owner.

### 6.2 Staff Access

Lumora staff may view footage only with the customer's explicit consent for support purposes. Consent is requested for a specific recording and a specific purpose, is recorded in the case and can be withdrawn at any time. Staff never download, copy or share customer footage outside the support system.

## 7 Privacy Incidents

A privacy incident is handled with urgency, whether it is reported by a customer, a member of staff or a partner.

### 7.1 Definition

Any report that personal data or footage may have been exposed - including a customer seeing another person's data - is a privacy incident. This includes misdirected emails, recordings visible to the wrong account, shared links that reached unintended people and data shared without consent. A report is treated as an incident until the assessment shows otherwise.

### 7.2 Logging

The incident is logged and the Privacy Officer notified within 24 hours. The log records what was reported, when and by whom, the data and customers that may be affected and the actions already taken. The complaint is escalated to Compliance Review under ESC-SOP-12 section 4.3.

### 7.3 Assessment

Compliance & Privacy completes an assessment within 72 hours. The assessment establishes what happened, which data and customers were affected, the risk to those customers and whether the regulator or affected customers must be notified under applicable data-protection law. The outcome is recorded in the incident log.

### 7.4 Containment

Containment actions: revoke shared links and sessions, disable affected features and preserve logs. Containment starts as soon as the incident is logged and does not wait for the assessment. Account Security supports containment where account access or sessions are involved, and the customer is told which steps have been taken to protect their data.

## 8 Prohibited Practices

The following practices are prohibited:

- disclosing another customer's data
- requesting unnecessary personal data
- promising the outcome of a regulatory assessment
- asking the customer to delete evidence

Customers who report an incident are asked to keep any screenshots or messages that show what they saw. Breaches of this section are reported to Compliance & Privacy.
