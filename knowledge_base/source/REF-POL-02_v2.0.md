# Refund Policy

Document ID: REF-POL-02 | Version: 2.0 | Status: Active | Effective date: 2026-01-01 | Expiry date: none | Owner: Returns & Refunds

## 1 Purpose

This policy defines when customers of Lumora Home Technologies are entitled to a refund, how refund requests are verified and processed, and the timelines that apply at each stage. It gives customers clear and consistent outcomes and gives staff an approved basis for every refund decision. This version replaces version 1.0 of this policy.

## 2 Scope

This policy applies to products and services purchased from the Lumora online store and Lumora Home app. It covers refunds for returned devices, defective products and deductions from refunds. Subscription cancellations and renewal refunds are governed by the Cancellation Policy CAN-POL-06 and the Subscription Services Policy SUB-POL-22. Products bought from other sellers are refunded under that seller's terms.

## 3 Refund Eligibility

Eligibility is assessed against the delivery date recorded for the order and the condition of the returned item. All periods in this section are counted in calendar days from the delivery date.

### 3.1 Standard Refund Window

Products may be returned for a refund within 30 calendar days of the delivery date. The item must be in resalable condition with all parts and accessories. Original packaging should be used where possible, and the return must be linked to the original order reference. Returns requested after the window has closed are not eligible under this section and can only be considered as a policy exception under section 7.1.

### 3.2 Care+ Members

Customers with an active Care+ plan have a 45 calendar day refund window. The Care+ plan must be active on the date the return is requested; a plan that was cancelled or had lapsed before that date does not extend the window. All other conditions of section 3.1 apply, and Care+ members are not charged the restocking fee described in section 6.1.

### 3.3 Defective Products

Defective products reported within 30 days of delivery qualify for a full refund or a replacement, at the customer's choice, after verification. Verification confirms the order, the reported fault and, where needed, the results of basic troubleshooting. Defects reported after 30 days are handled under the Warranty Policy WAR-POL-07. Defects that create a safety risk are always handled under the Product Safety Policy SAF-POL-10, whatever the age of the product.

### 3.4 Non-Refundable Items

The following are not refundable under this policy:

- completed Pro Install services (unless a service defect is confirmed under INS-POL-24)
- used subscription periods (see CAN-POL-06)
- gift cards
- products damaged by misuse

Products damaged by misuse may still be covered by Care+ accidental-damage cover under WAR-POL-07 section 6.2. Staff explain the reason when a request is declined and refer to the section that applies.

## 4 Refund Process

Refunds pass through four stages: verification, inspection of the returned item, issue of the refund and posting by the customer's bank. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

### 4.1 Verification Before Approval

A valid order reference and verification of the purchase are required before a refund is approved. Verification confirms that the order exists, that the item and amount match the request and that the request falls within the applicable window. Staff must not promise a refund before verification is complete. Until then, staff explain the eligibility conditions and the next step without committing to an outcome.

### 4.2 Inspection

Returned items are inspected within 3 business days of receipt. The inspection confirms the condition of the item, that all parts and accessories are present and, for defective products, that the reported fault is present. The result of the inspection is recorded in the case and determines whether the refund is approved in full, approved with a deduction under section 6 or declined.

### 4.3 Refund Issue Time

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of inspection approval. The customer is notified when the refund has been issued and receives the refund reference. Refunds are never issued to a different payment method, as store credit in place of the refund, or in cash.

### 4.4 Bank Posting

Bank posting can take up to 10 business days after the refund is issued; Lumora cannot guarantee bank posting times. Staff tell customers that the posting time depends on their bank or card provider and give the refund reference so that the customer can follow up with their bank if needed.

## 5 Refund Delays

This section applies when a refund is later than the timelines in section 4.

### 5.1 Late Refunds

If a refund has not been issued within the 5 business day target, Returns & Refunds investigates and gives the customer a status update within 1 business day. The target is the refund issue time in section 4.3. The update explains the cause of the delay and the corrective action being taken, without promising a date that has not been confirmed.

### 5.2 Refund Not Received

Refunds confirmed as issued but not received after 10 business days are traced by Billing Operations under BIL-POL-05 section 6. Returns & Refunds supports the trace by providing the refund reference, the amount and the date of issue. The customer is kept informed through the complaint until the trace is complete.

## 6 Partial Refunds and Fees

A refund may be reduced only for the reasons listed in this section, and every deduction is shown separately in the refund confirmation.

### 6.1 Restocking Fee

Opened, non-defective items returned after day 14 and within the refund window incur a 10% restocking fee. No restocking fee applies to defective, damaged-on-arrival or wrong items, or to Care+ members. Items returned unopened, or opened and returned within 14 days of delivery, are refunded without a restocking fee.

### 6.2 Shipping Fees

Original shipping fees are refunded only when the return is caused by a Lumora error (defective, damaged on arrival or wrong item). In all other cases the original shipping fee is not refunded. Return shipping for a Lumora error is covered by a prepaid return label issued under RPL-POL-03 section 4.2.

### 6.3 Deduction Disputes

Customers who dispute a deduction receive an itemised breakdown within 3 business days. The breakdown lists the amount paid, each deduction and the section of this policy that allows it. When a deduction is found to be incorrect, the difference is refunded to the original payment method.

## 7 Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy are rare and are decided only by the approvers named in this section.

### 7.1 Refunds Outside the Window

Refunds outside the eligibility window are policy exceptions and require Supervisor approval, or Department Manager approval above USD 500. The request is recorded with the reason for the exception and the amount involved, and the case is escalated under ESC-SOP-12 section 4.9. The customer is told that the request is under review and is not told that it will be approved.

### 7.2 No Agent Exceptions

Agents must not grant or promise policy exceptions. An agent who receives an exception request records it, explains that it will be reviewed by a supervisor and escalates the case. This applies even when the customer states that an exception was promised earlier; such statements are checked against the interaction records under STF-POL-21 section 4.2.

## 8 Prohibited Practices

The following practices are prohibited in all refund handling:

- promising a refund before verification
- refunding to a different payment method
- cash refunds
- requesting full card numbers

Payment details are verified using the last four digits of the card only (BIL-POL-05 section 8). Any breach of this section is reported to the team supervisor.
