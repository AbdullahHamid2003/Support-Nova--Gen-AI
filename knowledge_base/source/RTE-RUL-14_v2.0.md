# Department Routing Rules

Document ID: RTE-RUL-14 | Version: 2.0 | Status: Active | Effective date: 2026-01-01 | Expiry date: none | Owner: Management Escalations

## 1 Purpose

These rules define which Lumora Home Technologies department owns each complaint and which departments support it. Consistent routing ensures that every complaint reaches the team with the authority and expertise to resolve it, and that complaints with several issues involve every department that has an action to complete.

## 2 Routing Principles

The primary department owns the case; supporting departments contribute actions for secondary issues. Routing is decided by the subcategory of the primary issue (CHP-POL-01 section 3.2) using the routing table in section 3. The primary department is accountable for customer communication, the service level and closure; supporting departments complete their actions and record them in the case. Routing never depends on the customer type or on the tone of the complaint. The Lumora departments are:

| Code | Department | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| DEPT-BIL | Billing Operations | Charges, duplicate/incorrect billing, subscription billing, refund reconciliation and chargebacks. |
| DEPT-TEC | Technical Support | Device troubleshooting, connectivity, app/firmware problems, set-up and service outages. |
| DEPT-LOG | Logistics Support | Shipping, delivery delays, lost packages, wrong items and fulfilment errors (including missing parts). |
| DEPT-RET | Returns & Refunds | Returns, refund eligibility, refund processing, damaged-on-arrival replacements. |
| DEPT-WAR | Warranty Services | Warranty claims, denials, appeals and Care+ protection-plan claims. |
| DEPT-CRL | Customer Relations | Service quality, staff conduct, installation service quality and customer-experience recovery. |
| DEPT-SEC | Account Security | Login recovery, unauthorized access, account takeover, smart-lock security incidents and fraud. |
| DEPT-CMP | Compliance & Privacy | Privacy incidents, data-rights requests, consent, legal threats and regulatory matters. |
| DEPT-SAF | Product Safety | Overheating, fire, electrical hazards, injuries, recalls and hazardous-product collection. |
| DEPT-MGT | Management Escalations | Critical management escalations, high-value disputes and cross-department failures. |

## 4 Multi-Department Routing

The signals below add supporting departments to those listed in the routing table. A department is added only once, even when several signals apply, and a supporting department never replaces the primary department. A safety signal - a fire, overheating, an electrical hazard or an injury - in a complaint outside the Safety category adds Product Safety as a supporting department.

### 4.1 Secondary Issues

Each secondary issue adds its primary department as a supporting department. The primary department of a secondary issue is found by applying the routing table in section 3 to the subcategory of that issue. When that department is already the primary department of the case, it is not added again. The supporting department completes the actions required for its issue and records them in the case.

### 4.2 Privacy and Legal Signals

Privacy signals and legal threats add Compliance & Privacy as a supporting department. Privacy signals include any indication that personal data or footage may have been exposed or shared without consent. Legal threats include references to lawyers, courts or regulators. These signals also trigger Compliance Review under ESC-SOP-12 sections 4.3 and 4.4.

### 4.3 Critical Escalations

Critical Management Escalations add Management Escalations as a supporting department. This applies whenever a complaint is escalated to the Critical Management Escalation level under ESC-SOP-12 section 3.6, so that senior management can coordinate the response across all departments involved. Management Escalations records its decisions in the case and tells the primary department which actions it requires.

### 4.4 Security Signals

Security signals add Account Security as a supporting department. Security signals include unauthorised account access, account takeover, fraudulent payments and unexplained smart-lock events reported in a complaint that belongs to another category. Account Security then applies the containment steps in SEC-POL-09 section 4.

## 5 Routing Precedence

When the primary issue is unclear, precedence is Safety, then Security, then Privacy, then Legal, then Billing, then Product Defect, then Delivery, then Warranty, then all other categories. In this order, Security means unauthorised account access (ACC-UNA) and Legal means a legal threat in the complaint.

Root-cause issues outrank the refund consequences they cause: a product that arrived damaged and whose refund has not been processed has the primary issue Damaged on Arrival and the secondary issue Refund Delay. The issue with the highest precedence becomes the primary issue and decides the primary department; the other issues become secondary issues and add supporting departments under section 4.1.
