# Product Safety Policy

Document ID: SAF-POL-10 | Version: 2.0 | Status: Active | Effective date: 2025-08-15 | Expiry date: none | Owner: Product Safety

## 1 Purpose

This policy sets out how Lumora Home Technologies responds when a product may present a risk to people or property. Its aim is to protect customers first, to make sure every safety report reaches the Product Safety team quickly and to handle hazardous products and communications responsibly.

## 2 Scope

This policy applies to every Lumora device, accessory and battery, whatever its age, warranty status or place of purchase, and to every safety concern raised in a complaint, whichever category the complaint was first given. It applies to all staff who receive or handle complaints.

## 3 Safety Incident Definition

A safety incident is any report of overheating, smoke, burning smell, melting, swelling batteries, sparks, electric shock, fire or any injury involving a Lumora product. A report is treated as a safety incident even when the customer describes it calmly, mentions it only in passing or reports it as part of another issue. When staff are unsure whether a report describes a safety incident, they treat it as one.

## 4 Immediate Actions

The actions in this section are taken as soon as a safety incident is reported, before any other part of the complaint is handled.

### 4.1 Stop Using

Advise the customer to stop using the product and to disconnect it from power only if it is safe to do so. If there is fire, smoke or any immediate danger, the customer is told to move away from the product and contact local emergency services. The customer is asked not to try to open, repair or cool the product.

### 4.2 Incident Log

Log a safety incident within 1 hour. The log records the product, the order reference if available, what happened, whether anyone was injured, whether a child was involved and the advice given to the customer. Photos may be requested only when it is safe for the customer to take them.

### 4.3 Notify Product Safety

Notify the Product Safety team. The notification includes the incident log and the customer's contact details. Product Safety then leads the investigation and decides on collection of the product, testing and any wider action for the same product line. The owning department keeps the customer informed of each step agreed with Product Safety.

### 4.4 Escalation Level

Critical Management Escalation is mandatory when there is fire, an injury, or a child involved; otherwise escalate to the Specialist Team. The mandatory triggers in ESC-SOP-12 section 4.1 also apply; where a trigger there requires a higher level, for example for smoke, flames or explosion, the higher level applies (ESC-SOP-12 section 7).

## 5 Product Collection

Hazardous products are removed from the customer's home safely and are never sent through the ordinary returns process.

### 5.1 Specialist Collection

Product Safety arranges specialist collection of hazardous products. The collection is arranged with the customer at a time that suits them and uses packaging and transport approved for damaged batteries. The customer is not charged for the collection, and a replacement or refund is decided after the product has been assessed (section 6).

### 5.2 No Postal Returns of Batteries

Never instruct customers to post or courier a damaged, swollen or overheating lithium battery. This applies to the Lumora PowerCell Portable Power Station and to any other battery-powered product or loose battery. Prepaid return labels are not issued for these products; the collection in section 5.1 is used instead.

## 6 Remedy

After assessment, confirmed safety defects receive a full refund or replacement regardless of warranty status or refund window. The customer chooses between the two. Product Safety confirms the outcome of the assessment in writing, and Returns & Refunds or Warranty Services carries out the remedy.

## 7 Communication

Safety communications are factual, supportive and careful.

### 7.1 No Liability Statements

Do not admit liability or speculate about the cause. Staff acknowledge what the customer has reported, explain the actions being taken and the next step, and leave conclusions about the cause to the Product Safety assessment. On injury cases, communications also follow any guidance given by Compliance & Privacy under section 7.3.

### 7.2 Never Dismiss

Never dismiss or minimise a reported safety concern. Staff do not suggest that heat, smoke, sparks or a burning smell are normal, and do not advise the customer to keep using the product while the concern is being investigated. Every report is logged and passed to Product Safety, even when the customer says the problem has stopped.

### 7.3 Injury Review

Injury cases are reviewed by Compliance. Compliance & Privacy is added to the case as soon as an injury is reported and reviews the communications and the handling of the case, in line with CMP-GDL-19. Any compensation connected with an injury requires Compliance approval under CPN-POL-11 section 8.

## 8 Recalls

Lumora may issue a recall or safety notice for a product line when Product Safety identifies a risk.

### 8.1 Recall Complaints

Complaints that mention a recall notice reach Product Safety within 4 hours. This applies whether the customer is asking whether their product is affected, reporting a problem with a recalled product or complaining about the recall process. Recall-related complaints are escalated to the Specialist Team under ESC-SOP-12 section 4.14.
