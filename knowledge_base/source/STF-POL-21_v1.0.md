# Staff Conduct and Service Standards

Document ID: STF-POL-21 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Active | Effective date: 2025-06-01 | Expiry date: none | Owner: Customer Relations

## 1 Purpose

This policy sets the standards of conduct and service that Lumora Home Technologies expects from every member of its support staff and every technician, and explains how complaints about staff are handled. It protects customers from poor treatment and incorrect information, and protects staff by making expectations clear.

## 2 Conduct Standards

Staff are respectful, accurate and never make commitments outside policy. They treat every customer with courtesy and patience, including customers who are upset or angry, and they never use offensive, dismissive or discriminatory language. Staff protect customer data, identify themselves by first name and team, and are honest when they do not know an answer and need to check it.

## 3 Support Service Standards

The standards in this section describe the service that customers can expect from every support contact.

### 3.1 First-Contact Resolution

Staff aim to resolve a complaint in the first contact when the policy allows it and all the required information is available. When that is not possible, they explain what will happen next and who will handle it, so that the customer does not have to repeat their complaint.

### 3.2 Warm Transfers

When a complaint must be passed to another team, staff make a warm transfer: they explain the case to the receiving team before the customer is transferred, or record a complete summary in the case for written channels. Customers are not asked to repeat information that has already been recorded.

### 3.3 Callback Commitments

A callback is offered only when a time can be agreed with the customer. The agreed time is recorded in the case, and the callback is made at that time. If the agent who agreed the callback is not available, another member of the team makes the call.

## 4 Accuracy of Information

Customers rely on what Lumora staff tell them, so every statement about a policy, a timeline or an outcome must be accurate.

### 4.1 No Out-of-Policy Commitments

Staff must not make commitments outside policy. This includes promising refunds, replacements, compensation, exceptions or delivery dates that the applicable policy does not support or that have not yet been verified. When a customer asks for something the policy does not allow, staff explain the policy and, where appropriate, the escalation route.

### 4.2 Correcting Misinformation

Incorrect information given by staff is corrected; a commitment is honoured only when it is within policy, otherwise the case goes to Supervisor Review. The customer receives the correct information with an apology for the error. The interaction record is checked first to confirm what was said.

## 5 Complaints About Staff

Complaints about staff are handled by Customer Relations, fairly for both the customer and the member of staff.

### 5.1 Record Review

Interaction records are reviewed within 5 business days. The review covers call recordings, chat transcripts, emails and visit notes as relevant. The member of staff's manager is informed of the complaint, and the member of staff has the opportunity to give their account.

### 5.2 Outcome Communication

The customer is informed of the outcome without disclosure of disciplinary details. The message confirms that the complaint has been reviewed, what Lumora has done to put things right for the customer and, where relevant, that the matter has been addressed with the member of staff.

## 6 Serious Misconduct

Serious misconduct is escalated as follows:

- Harassment, discrimination or threats: Compliance Review.
- Theft or property damage: Department Manager.

These escalations match ESC-SOP-12 section 4.10. Property damage by technicians is also investigated under INS-POL-24 section 6. The customer's safety and the preservation of records come first.
