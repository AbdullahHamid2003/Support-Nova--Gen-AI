# Warranty Policy

Document ID: WAR-POL-07 | Version: 2.0 | Status: Active | Effective date: 2025-07-01 | Expiry date: none | Owner: Warranty Services

## 1 Purpose

This policy describes the manufacturer warranty that Lumora Home Technologies provides for its devices, the extended cover available through the Lumora Care+ Protection Plan, how warranty claims are made and decided, and how customers can appeal a decision. It gives customers a clear and fair remedy when a device fails in normal use.

## 2 Scope

This policy applies to Lumora devices purchased new from the Lumora online store and the Lumora Home app. It applies to faults that appear more than 30 days after delivery; earlier defects are handled under the Refund Policy REF-POL-02 section 3.3 and the Replacement and Returns Policy RPL-POL-03. Subscriptions and services are not covered by the device warranty.

### 3.1 12-Month Products

The standard warranty for the following products is 12 months from delivery: Sentinel Indoor Camera, Sentinel Outdoor Camera, Glide Robot Vacuum, Halo Smart Speaker, Spark Smart Plug, Beam Smart Bulb. Multi-packs are covered per unit, so each plug or bulb in a pack has its own warranty running from the same delivery date. Vacuum brushes and filters are consumable parts and are excluded under section 5.

### 3.2 24-Month Products

The standard warranty for the following products is 24 months from delivery: Aura Smart Thermostat, PowerCell Portable Power Station, Keystone Smart Lock, Nexus Home Hub, Breeze Air Purifier. These products are typically installed or relied on for longer periods and therefore carry the longer warranty. A replacement device supplied under this policy is covered for the remainder of the original warranty period.

## 4 Claim Process

Claims are made through the Lumora Home app or by contacting support and are decided by Warranty Services. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

### 4.1 Claim Requirements

A warranty claim requires proof of purchase (order reference), a description of the fault and completion of basic troubleshooting (TEC-GDL-20). A claim is complete when all three have been provided. When information is missing, Warranty Services asks for it with focused questions and the claim is decided once it has been received. Photos or a short video of the fault may be requested.

### 4.2 Decision Time

Warranty Services decides a complete claim within 10 business days. The decision is sent to the customer in writing and explains the remedy or, if the claim is declined, the reason and the exclusion in section 5 that applies. The customer is also told how to appeal under section 7.

### 4.3 Remedies

Remedies are repair, replacement, or refund where repair or replacement is not possible. Warranty Services chooses the remedy based on the fault and the availability of parts and stock. A refund under this section is issued to the original payment method for the price paid for the device.

## 5 Exclusions

The warranty covers faults that arise in normal use. It does not cover:

- physical or liquid damage
- unauthorised repair or modification
- normal wear such as vacuum brushes and filters
- cosmetic damage that does not affect function

Accidental physical or liquid damage may be covered by Care+ under section 6.2. Safety-related defects are never excluded (section 8).

## 6 Care+ Protection Plan

Lumora Care+ is a subscription described in the Subscription Services Policy SUB-POL-22. It can be added within 30 days of device purchase (SUB-POL-22 section 7), and its benefits apply only while the plan is active.

### 6.1 Extended Coverage

Care+ extends coverage to 36 months from delivery. The extended coverage applies to every Lumora device enrolled in the member's Care+ plan and follows the same claim process and exclusions as the standard warranty, except where section 6.2 provides accidental-damage cover.

### 6.2 Accidental Damage

Care+ covers up to 2 accidental-damage claims per 12 months with a USD 29 service fee per claim. Accidental damage includes drops and liquid spills that happen in normal household use. Deliberate damage, loss and theft are not covered. The service fee is charged when the claim is approved.

### 6.3 Advance Replacement

Care+ members receive advance replacement under RPL-POL-03 section 4.3. The replacement device ships before the faulty device is returned, and the faulty device is then returned with a prepaid label, except for damaged battery products, which are collected under SAF-POL-10 section 5.

## 7 Appeals

Customers who disagree with a warranty decision can ask for it to be reviewed.

### 7.1 Appeal Window

Customers may appeal a warranty denial within 30 days of the decision. The appeal can be made by replying to the decision or through a new complaint, and the customer may add any evidence that was not available when the claim was decided.

### 7.2 Appeal Review

Appeals are reviewed by a Warranty Services supervisor (Supervisor Review) within 10 business days. The supervisor reviews the original claim, the evidence and the exclusion that was applied, and the customer receives the appeal outcome in writing with the reasons for it.

## 8 Safety-Related Defects

Safety-related defects are handled under SAF-POL-10 regardless of warranty status. A device that overheats, smokes, sparks or causes an electric shock is treated as a safety incident, even when the warranty has expired or an exclusion would otherwise apply. Warranty Services supports Product Safety with the device history.
